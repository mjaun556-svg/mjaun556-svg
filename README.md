# Mohd Jaun Rizvi

Crypto quant developer and researcher at an HFT firm, based in Bengaluru. Open to quant developer and quant researcher roles.

I work across the whole loop: market data feeds from 10+ venues, real-time trading and risk tools, and research on market making and relative value strategies using real L2 order book data.

### Areas

- Market making and execution: fill simulation, queue position, adverse selection, fees
- Market microstructure: L1/L2 order books, funding and basis across CEX, DEX and prediction markets
- Relative value: perp vs dated futures basis, calendar spreads, cross-venue spreads
- Backtesting: realistic fills, data checks, walk-forward and out-of-sample testing
- Infrastructure: WebSocket feed handlers, dashboards, 24/7 alerting
